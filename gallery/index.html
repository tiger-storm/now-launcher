Start with your home

play_arrow

Get Started

Follow the welcome screen, explore the basics, choose your default search and set up Sections. Tap Finish to arrive at your new home.

play_arrow

Your day in Sections

Sections bring apps, contacts, calendar, media, reminders and search together. Free home uses your chosen order. Sections remain available through Search when you use Pro.

play_arrow

Arrange your home

Open Edit to organise your Sections. Choose which ones appear, change their order and explore layouts with live previews, so you can see your home take shape.

Get things done

play_arrow

Every app, a swipe away

Swipe down to open All Apps, browse your installed apps or narrow the list with search. All Apps is available in both Free and Pro; this recording starts from a Pro home.

play_arrow

Find it with Search

Activate Search to open Sections, then start typing to see results. In Pro, dismissing Search brings you back to contextual Suggestions. Pro also adds advanced search actions. This recording demonstrates the Pro flow.

play_arrow

Keep track of what matters

See reminders on your home, open a checklist and mark tasks complete. The recording shows completing tasks with sample reminders. In Pro, reminders can also appear in Suggestions.

play_arrow

Turn a thought into a reminder

Type what you need to remember and when. Advanced search prepares a reminder draft so you can review the details before tapping Create. The reminder is saved only when you choose.

play_arrow

Controls within reach

Tap the Now avatar to open Quick Settings. Reach brightness, volume and other everyday controls, then return to your home. Some controls depend on device support and permissions.

Make your icons your own

Change the character of your app icons with shapes and themed colours. Application settings also include icon-pack options. Themed icon availability depends on app support.

Discover Pro

play_arrow

A home that follows your day

Pro replaces the default Sections home with contextual Suggestions. See useful information throughout your day, while keeping Sections a search activation away. These examples use sample times and information.

photo

Choose what appears

Choose which types of Suggestions appear, independently of your Sections visibility. Some presentation settings are shared. This screenshot previews Suggestions settings; the walkthrough is still to come.

play_arrow

Add something of your own

Create your own Suggestions for places, people, apps and more. Choose the details and when they should appear. This recording demonstrates creating a custom Maps suggestion with sample information.
